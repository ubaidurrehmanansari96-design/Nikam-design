About The Studio

Designed around the way you live.

Nikkam Designs StudioX creates refined interiors where architecture, materials, lighting and functionality come together seamlessly.

Based in Bengaluru, our approach prioritizes spatial clarity, tactility, and quiet elegance—crafting private sanctuaries and distinguished commercial spaces that stand outside transient trends.

“Quiet luxury lies in the harmony of light, pure geometry, and honest materials.”

Studio Philosophy

Less, but never ordinary.

We believe that true luxury does not demand attention; it commands presence through quiet perfection. Every curve, shadow, and tactile material is thoughtfully orchestrated.

Clean Architectural Forms

Pure geometries and balanced proportions that frame uncluttered, tranquil environments.

Timeless Materials

Honest materials—natural stones, warm hardwoods, patinated metals—that age gracefully.

Natural Light

Sculpting rooms around light, shadows, and time of day to enhance mood and spatial depth.

Functional Luxury

Intelligent spatial flows that elevate daily routines into effortless sensory experiences.

Refined Detailing

Invisible flush joints, seamless transitions, and immaculate custom craftsmanship.

Personalised Spaces

Interiors intimately calibrated around individual lifestyles, values, and architectural contexts.

Our Methodology

The Design Journey

01

DISCOVER

Understand the client’s lifestyle, spatial requirements, functional desires, and overarching vision.

02

DEFINE

Develop initial spatial planning, architectural moodboards, layout studies, and material direction.

03

DESIGN

Refine every detail through precise CAD drawings, bespoke millwork details, finish schedules, and 3D visualisations.

04

DELIVER

Coordinate meticulous on-site execution, vendor management, and bring the designed space seamlessly to life.
